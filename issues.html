American Frontier

Operation Red Burrow

During the first Democratic primary debate Gov. Chicken Joe stated "He belongs on Mars with the rest of the mole rats". Graham believes the mole rats should be treated better. Naked mole rats can survive for around 18 minutes without any oxygen by changing their brains to burn fructose (Science, 2017). Researchers from the University of Rochester have been looking into why these animals almost never get cancer and can live for over 30 years making them the longest living rodent. This is exactly what astronauts will face on Mars, as the atmosphere is extremely thin and consists of around 95% carbon dioxide. Long duration missions expose crews to radiation increasing their risk of cancer. Operation Red Burrow will tell NASA to send a small group of naked mole rats on an early Mars cargo mission. Animals have been sent into space for many years including Laika (1957) and Ham the chimpanzee (1961). NASA have had many Rodent Research missions on the International Space Station, so as Earth and Mars line up to provide an efficient journey only around once every 26 months, Graham will ask NASA to aim to have the first launch window during his term in office. He admits this will also prove Joe and his team wrong.

Frontier Builders

In April 2026 NASA’s Artemis II crew orbited the moon, the first humans to do so since 1972. NASA plan to land astronauts close to the South Pole of the moon during Artemis IV in 2028. However landing is only the beginning. Astronauts will need shelters that can be built, maintained and run from a distance with no access to spare parts. Graham’s Frontier Builders initiative will ask volunteer Amish & Mennonite craftsmen to work with NASA engineers to design and build prototype habitats. Amish communities have been building strong homes and barns using simple methods that are easy to fix for generations. During a traditional Amish barn raising hundreds of neighbours can build the frame and roof of an entire barn in one day, this is exactly what is needed for low tech building in space. The Amish population in North America has increased to around 421,200 in 2026, an increase of 32% since 2017. Nearly 60% live in Ohio, Pennsylvania and Indiana, meaning Graham will be able to meet and work with local builders. Everyone will volunteer and will be based around the methods used within each community. Heavy lift rockets such as Starship (first reached orbit on 28th September 2026) are expected to deliver the final designs to the moon and mars. NASA plan to retire the International Space Station around 2030 using an $843 million deorbit vehicle from SpaceX. Graham wants American habitats on the moon.

Graham will put Americans first on Mars with habitats built by Americans.
