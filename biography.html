Meet Graham

A Youngstown kid who served his country, built a business from nothing and went on to lead Ohio.

GRAHAM L. JANICIK
His Story

Where Graham comes from

Graham Lawrence Janicik was born in 1972 in Youngstown Ohio, a steel hotspot that was starting to see some of its mills disappear and the economy going downhill rapidly. On the 19th September 1977 (Black Monday to locals) Graham was 5 years old when Youngstown Sheet & Tube announced that it was closing down its Campbell Steelworks, leaving over 5000 people out of work. This included his dad Dale, who went on to drive commercial trucks. His other dad, Marcus, worked a stable job as a nurse, however the family did suffer from times where they had to use food stamps and food donations from the local church. At 12 Graham began cutting grass to help with bills and other costs around the house. His parents put the values of working hard, keeping your word and looking after your family in him. These are values that will stay with him in his higher office.

At 18 Graham joined the U.S Army to escape his struggling hometown and get some money back home to support his family. Graham became a Combat Engineer and worked during the start of Operation Desert Storm in 1991. During this time he helped to clear routes for American tanks in the Iraqi desert, removing barbed wire and homemade defences. After returning to Ohio Graham used the GI Bill from the government to gain an Engineering Technology Degree from Youngstown State University. He returned home to his family as a first generation college graduate and independent young man. In 1999 Graham borrowed heavily against his car to create Janicik Fabrication. It started off as a two man steel company in an empty warehouse in Campbell. It struggled at first but eventually got on its feet. Over the next fifteen years Graham grew it to a company with 300 union supported employees producing steel for bridges, hospitals and schools throughout Ohio and the Midwest. During this time he married Brittany, a school nurse, and they have four children together.

Janicik never planned on entering any office however after seeing too many of his favourite neighbours leave Ohio to find proper work, he knew that someone needed to speak up for those who could only afford to stay. As a two term Governor of Ohio he balanced the budget without increasing state income tax. During the height of the modern Fentanyl crisis he increased addiction treatments throughout all 88 counties. This was a massive improvement to local, state and even national recreational drug protocals and responses. He also brought new manufacturing and energy jobs to communities that had been forgotten about for years. Graham brought his army, shop floor and hard working teen in the 80s values with him to his office. Being punctual, listening to those working however never asking anyone to do something that you wouldn’t be comfortable doing. Graham was re-elected by republicans, democrats and independents to serve a second term in Columbus. He is now being pushed to take up the Presidency and bring the United States back to prosperity.

Graham truly believes that America’s best days are ahead of us and can be achieved in one term. He knows that kids growing up in small towns with two dads, single moms, grand parents or in foster care should have the same opportunities as rich kids or senators sons. He believes that parents should be able to purchase a home, fill up their tank or save for retirement with one solid pay packet. Men and women serving our country should have a government that keeps their promises and really has a connection with the common soldier. He believes we have much more in common as Americans than the politicians in Washington would like us to believe. He is running for President to bring some common sense back to the white house and America.

The Family

Faith, family, and the Mahoning Valley

Graham and Brittany have been married for 26 years and still live in Boardman, the same area where they raised their four kids. Emily is a Navy nurse, Ryan is a union electrician, Jackson teaches high school and Grace is finishing up at Ohio State. They are also grandparents to two. Most Sundays Graham goes to St. Columba Cathedral and then has a cookout with his dads, and he hasn't missed a Youngstown State Penguins home opener in 25 years.
