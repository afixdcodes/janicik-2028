The Mission

Make America Work Again for Working Families

Every one of Graham's plans starts with the same question: does this make life better for the family at the kitchen table in Youngstown, Erie, or Green Bay? That means groceries and gas people can afford, power bills that don't double, a border that's secure, and factories that hire again. Graham balanced budgets and brought jobs back to Ohio. Now he's ready to do it for the country.
