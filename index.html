The Mission

Make America Work Again for Working Families

Every plan Graham puts forward starts with one question, will this make life better for families in places like Youngstown, Erie and Green Bay? That means affordable groceries and gas, power bills that don't keep going up, a secure border and factories that are hiring again. Graham balanced the budget and brought jobs back to Ohio and now he is ready to do the same for America.
